Our story

Fifteen years at the bench

JS Jewelry is a small custom studio inside Asian Garden Mall in Westminster. We have spent fifteen years designing and making fine jewelry, and every piece still starts the same way: a conversation about what it should mean to the person wearing it.

We choose every diamond for the balance of fire and brilliance, because cut and proportion matter as much as color and clarity. That goes for the center stone and for the smallest accent in the band. It is the difference you see from across the room.

Lab-grown or natural? Both are real diamonds. We walk you through the difference honestly and let you choose what is right for you. No pressure either way.
