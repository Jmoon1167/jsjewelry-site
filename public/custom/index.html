How a commission works

01

Tell us what you're picturing

A screenshot, a sketch, a stone you already own, or a heirloom you want reset. Rough is fine. Most people start with rough.

02

We design it in CAD

A $200 design deposit covers the hours of hands-on work to model your piece in our CAD program. It is credited toward your final price, so it is never money lost.

03

You approve, we cast

Once you approve the CAD design and the price, a 50% deposit starts casting, stone setting and finishing.

04

Finished and shipped

Most pieces take 5 to 10 business days once your design and price are approved. We photograph your finished piece, you pay the balance in full, and we ship it free by next-day air, fully insured.
